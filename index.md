# Privacy Policy for VibeLand: Puzzle Games

**Last updated:** November 13, 2026

VibeLand: Puzzle Games ("the app") is an Android app containing puzzle games, currently **BoltVibe** and **ArrowVibe** ("the games"). It is published by **Muhammad Taimoor** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and how. If you have questions, contact us at **muhamadtaimoor1122@gmail.com**.

## 1. Summary

- We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
- Your game progress stays on your device.
- The games show ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device and usage information, described below.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so each game can remember where you are. Each game keeps its own separate data:

- Level progress, stars and unlocked levels
- Coins, booster counts and daily reward status
- Settings such as sound and vibration

We do not receive this information, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The games display banner ads, full-screen (interstitial) ads and optional rewarded video ads using **Google AdMob**. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and Google may automatically collect and use:

- Advertising ID and other device identifiers
- IP address and approximate location derived from it
- Device information (model, operating system, language)
- App interactions with ads (impressions, clicks, rewarded views)
- Diagnostics such as crash and performance data

This information is collected and processed by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you personally.

You can control ad personalisation at any time in your Android settings (**Settings → Google → Ads**), where you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under **Settings → Privacy & ad choices**.

## 4. Permissions

The app uses only the permissions it needs:

- **Internet / network state:** to load ads
- **Vibration:** to provide haptic feedback (you can turn this off in Settings)

## 5. How we use information

We use the information above only to run the games, show and measure ads, keep the app working properly, and comply with the law. We do not sell your personal information.

## 6. Sharing

We do not share information with anyone except the advertising service described in Section 3, and where required by law.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. Data held by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's policies. Because we do not run accounts or collect your personal data ourselves, we have nothing to delete on our side. To ask any question about your data, email **muhamadtaimoor1122@gmail.com**.

## 8. Children's privacy

VibeLand is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will help resolve it.

## 9. Security

We keep our own data collection to a minimum. Data sent between the app and ad services is encrypted in transit. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may add more games to the app over time. If a new game or feature uses information in a way not described here, we will update this policy before it is released. We may also update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
